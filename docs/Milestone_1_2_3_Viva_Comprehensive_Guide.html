1. Project Overview & Quick Elevator Pitch

Project One-Liner for Viva:
"Our project is an AI-Based Patient Appointment System that bridges the gap between medical symptom self-assessment and doctor appointment scheduling. It features a 24/7 conversational AI triage chatbot that analyzes patient symptoms, detects medical emergencies, recommends the exact medical specialist (Cardiologist, Neurologist, etc.), prevents double-booking through atomic scheduling, and provides doctors with AI-generated pre-consultation clinical summaries."

High-Level Problem It Solves

  • Patient Self-Diagnosis Problem: Patients often don't know which doctor to consult (e.g. visiting an Orthopedist for joint pain caused by Rheumatic fever or visiting a General Physician for acute neurological symptoms), leading to wasted appointments and delayed treatment.
  • Disconnection in Current Apps: Apps like Practo/Zocdoc book appointments but don't do AI symptom diagnosis. Symptom checkers like Ada Health diagnose symptoms but don't book nearby doctors. Our system unifies both!
  • Doctor Context Deficiency: Doctors currently spend 10–15 minutes asking basic preliminary questions. Our system generates a ready-to-read pre-consultation summary before the patient walks in.
  • Emergency Overrides: Patients with acute life-threatening symptoms (heart attack, stroke) are immediately redirected to Emergency Services (ER) rather than booking normal clinic slots.
How will you introduce the project to the examiner in 30 seconds?
"Good morning Sir/Ma'am. Our project is an AI-Based Patient Appointment System. In existing platforms, patients either self-diagnose and choose the wrong doctor on booking apps like Practo, or they use symptom checkers like Ada Health without direct appointment booking. Our platform combines conversational AI symptom triage, emergency red-flag detection, automatic doctor-specialty recommendation, conflict-free real-time slot booking, and pre-consultation AI clinical summaries for doctors on a unified, secure dashboard."

4.3 The 2-Tier AI Recommendation Algorithm

Tier 1: Emergency Classifier (Red-Flag Filter)
High-priority token set: ["chest pain", "shortness of breath", "slurred speech", "numbness in face", "arm pain", "stroke", "heart attack", "unconscious", "severe bleeding", "difficulty breathing"].
If any emergency token is present → Triggers instant EMERGENCY WARNING, instructs the patient to call emergency services (112/108/911), and bypasses elective clinic booking for patient safety.
Tier 2: Tokenized Jaccard Similarity Matcher & Confidence Calculation
For non-emergency inputs, the AI engine tokenizes the user input tokens $U$ and dataset symptom tokens $S$: $$\text{Similarity Score} = \frac{|U \cap S|}{\max(|S|, 1)}$$ $$\text{Confidence} = \min\left(0.60 + (\text{Similarity Score} \times 0.35),\, 0.95\right)$$ Safe Default Fallback: If similarity score across all 12 specialties is $\le 0.15$, it routes safely to a General Physician with baseline confidence of 70%.

What algorithm did you use in Milestone 2 for symptom assessment?
"We built a Two-Tier Hybrid AI Triage Engine in Python. Tier 1 executes deterministic emergency keyword detection (for red flags like chest pain or slurred speech) to immediately warn patients and redirect to emergency care. 5.3 The 8 Subsystem Modules

1. Auth & Identity Service

Google OAuth 2.0 GIS token verification, OTP generator (SMS/Email), Bcrypt password hashing, JWT access/refresh tokens.

2. AI Chatbot Drawer

Interactive drawer sliding in from the right, multi-turn conversational symptom intake, instant safety badge rendering.

3. Clinical Assessment Engine

Parses unstructured text, scores against 20+ disease clusters, detects emergency conditions, calculates confidence level.

4. Doctor Recommendation Service

Filters verified doctors matching the diagnosed specialty, sorted by rating, fees, and location proximity.

5. Doctor Schedule Management

Granular 30-minute consultation slots, dynamic availability checks, doctor leave handling.

6. Appointment Booking Engine

Atomic transaction booking, duplicate slot prevention, unique appointment tracking IDs.

7. Patient Health Records

Patient consultation history, symptoms logged, AI assessment history, active/past status.

8. Doctor Clinical Dashboard

Live queue of scheduled patients, AI-generated clinical pre-briefings, consultation status toggles.

How does your system prevent two patients from booking the same doctor slot?
"We enforce Atomic Slot Locking in the backend booking service and database constraints. When an appointment request is received, the backend checks for existing active appointments matching `(doctor_id, date, slot)`. If found, it rejects the duplicate transaction with an HTTP 409 Conflict error. In the UI, booked slots are dynamically disabled in real time."

6. Master Viva Questions & Answers (Top 15 Expected Questions)

Q1: What is the main objective of your project?
Answer: The main objective is to provide an end-to-end intelligent healthcare platform that uses AI to analyze patient symptoms, detect emergencies, recommend the right medical specialist, enable seamless real-time appointment booking without slot conflicts, and provide doctors with structured AI clinical summaries before consultation.
Q2: What is the difference between Milestone 1, 2, and 3?
Answer:
  • Milestone 1: Problem definition, market study of existing systems (Practo, Ada), identifying research gaps, and writing Functional/Non-Functional Requirements.
  • Milestone 2: Dataset collection from clinical taxonomies, 6-stage data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and creating the 2-tier AI triage engine.
  • Milestone 3: Full system architecture, technology stack selection, 8-module decomposition, relational ER database modeling, and Google OAuth 2.0 / JWT security design.
Q3: Which technologies are you using across the stack and why?
Answer:
  • Frontend: React 18 + Vite for high performance, modular components, and responsive modern UI.
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python) because it is asynchronous (`asyncio`), ultra-fast, has built-in Pydantic validation, and auto-generates Swagger API documentation.
  • Database: SQLite for development and PostgreSQL for production with SQLAlchemy ORM for relational integrity.
  • AI Engine: Custom Python NLP triage module using tokenized Jaccard similarity and deterministic red-flag emergency rule matching.
  • Authentication: Google OAuth 2.0 (Google Identity Services) + JWT tokens + Bcrypt for password security.
Q4: How does your AI triage handle medical emergencies?
Answer: We have a dedicated Tier 1 Emergency Classifier that checks for critical red-flag keywords (such as severe chest pain, shortness of breath, slurred speech, sudden facial numbness). When detected, it immediately displays an Emergency Red Alert instructing the patient to call emergency services (112/108/911) or visit the nearest ER immediately, completely overriding elective clinic booking.
Q5: How does the AI calculate confidence for doctor recommendations?
Answer: We calculate the token overlap (Jaccard similarity) between user symptom tokens and our validated clinical knowledge base. The confidence score is mapped dynamically between 60% and 95%: $\text{Confidence} = \min(0.60 + (\text{Similarity} \times 0.35), 0.95)$. If the similarity is $\le 0.15$, it routes safely to a General Physician with 70% confidence.
Q6: What happens if the patient types symptoms that don't match any specialist?
Answer: The system has a safe fallback mechanism. If no specific specialist matches above our threshold (≤ 15% similarity), the AI routes the patient to a General Physician (Internal Medicine), which is the standard medical protocol for non-specific symptoms.
Q7: How is patient safety and legal compliance addressed?
Answer:
  1. Every AI assessment contains a statutory disclaimer: "This is a preliminary AI triage assessment and does not constitute a definitive medical diagnosis or prescription."
  2. Doctor-in-the-Loop: All AI assessments are provided to the doctor as a draft summary for clinical verification.
  3. Data privacy: Encrypted transit (TLS) and rest (AES-256) with role-based access control (RBAC).
Q8: How does the Google Sign-In authentication work?
Answer: We use Google Identity Services (GIS) OAuth 2.0. The frontend receives a signed Google ID credential (JWT) from Google and sends it to our backend endpoint /api/auth/google. The backend verifies the cryptographic signature with Google's public certificates, extracts the user's email, name, and profile, and creates/retrieves the user in our database while issuing our own secure JWT session token.
Q9: How do doctors benefit from your system?
Answer: Doctors get a dedicated Doctor Dashboard showing their daily appointment queue, patient details, and most importantly, an AI-generated pre-consultation summary of reported symptoms and urgency level. This saves 5–10 minutes of manual inquiry per patient and allows doctors to focus on clinical examination.
Q10: What are the future milestones (Milestones 4 & 5)?
Answer:
  • Milestone 4: Complete frontend and backend implementation, interactive chatbot UI, slot booking modal, and doctor dashboard.
  • Milestone 5: Comprehensive system testing, security audit, latency benchmarking, edge case validation, and final project deployment.
