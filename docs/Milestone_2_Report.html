Executive Summary & Milestone 2 Scope

The diagnostic accuracy, safety, and doctor routing reliability of the AI-Based Patient Appointment System depend entirely on high-quality medical knowledge bases. In traditional systems, patients often self-select inappropriate specialties due to lack of medical knowledge. Milestone 2 establishes the foundational data architecture by collecting, cleaning, preprocessing, and analyzing comprehensive healthcare datasets.

Milestone 2 Core Accomplishments

  • Healthcare Dataset Curation: Sourced multi-symptom condition mappings linking patient-reported sensations to verified clinical conditions and specialist disciplines.
  • End-to-End Preprocessing Pipeline: Implemented automated tokenization, deduplication, missing value handling, text normalization, and clinical synonym harmonization.

Healthcare Dataset Sourcing & Taxonomy

Data integrity and clinical safety require authoritative sourcing. Datasets were extracted and cross-referenced from validated clinical knowledge bases including Columbia University Medical Center Disease-Symptom Repositories, Mayo Clinic Clinical Guidelines, and ICD-10 Categorization Standards.

2.1 Symptom-Disease Knowledge Base Schema (symptom_specialist_dataset.csv)

Field Name Data Type Description & Clinical Function Sample Value
symptoms String (CSV) Normalized clinical tokens and symptom keywords "chest pain, shortness of breath, left arm pain"
disease String Standard medical condition / pathology diagnosis Heart Attack
recommended_specialist String Target medical doctor specialization discipline Cardiologist
severity Enum (L/M/H) Urgency level (Low, Medium, High) High
is_emergency Boolean Rapid override flag for immediate Emergency Room (ER) routing True

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing Pipeline

Patient symptom inputs frequently contain spelling variations, colloquial terms, and grammatical noise. The developed preprocessing pipeline transforms raw text into standardized clinical tokens ready for similarity matching.

Figure 1: Multi-Stage Healthcare Data Preprocessing & Cleaning Architecture
Data Cleaning Operations
  • Null Pruning: Discarded incomplete symptom tuples.
  • Deduplication: Merged redundant symptom variations.
  • Synonym Mapping: Standardized terms like "dyspnea" → "shortness of breath".
Emergency Keyword Filter Registry
chest pain, shortness of breath, slurred speech, numbness in face, heart attack, stroke, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness, severe bleeding.

AI Engine Integration & Similarity Scoring

The backend AI engine (backend/app/services/ai_engine.py) executes a two-tier hybrid assessment for all patient interactions:

Figure 3: AI Symptom Assessment & Clinical Triage Flow
Dynamic Similarity & Confidence Formula:
Similarity Score = |User_Tokens ∩ Dataset_Tokens| / max(|Dataset_Tokens|, 1)
Confidence Score = min(0.60 + (Similarity Score × 0.35), 0.95)
