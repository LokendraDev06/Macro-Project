Executive Summary & Milestone 3 Scope

Building upon the clinical datasets, exploratory analyses, and preprocessing pipelines formalized in Milestone 2, Milestone 3 establishes the holistic software architecture and subsystem module design of the AI-Based Patient Appointment System. In modern healthcare delivery, operational inefficiencies frequently arise because patient registration, symptom intake, clinical triage, doctor availability tracking, and medical consultation histories exist in disjointed silos.

Milestone 3 synthesizes these disparate components into a cohesive, high-performance, asynchronous multi-tier architecture. It explicitly formulates the system contracts, database schemas, API pathways, and data flows connecting the intelligent conversational chatbot to patient profiles, physician appointment books, and provider consultation portals.

Comprehensive Module Decomposition

The system is decomposed into eight highly specialized functional modules with clearly delineated boundaries, input/output contracts, and responsibilities:

1. Patient Registration & Dual-Auth Service
Provides seamless patient onboarding via Google OAuth 2.0 (Google Identity Services) or Mobile/Email OTP verification. Employs Bcrypt password hashing and issues signed JWT bearer tokens for secure session maintenance.
2. AI Health Chatbot Conversational Drawer
An interactive conversational interface allowing patients to describe complaints in unstructured natural language. Guides dialogue, prompts for duration/severity, and delivers structured preliminary recommendations.
3. Symptom Assessment & Emergency Engine
Evaluates symptoms against high-risk clinical triggers (e.g. cardiac distress, stroke symptoms). Non-emergency queries are matched against the medical dataset to identify probable health conditions and severity tiers.
4. Doctor Recommendation & Specialist Router
Translates the evaluated condition into targeted medical specialties (Cardiology, Dermatology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pulmonology, General Medicine) to eliminate patient self-triage misdirection.
5. Doctor Availability & Slot Management Matrix
Maintains real-time consultation calendars for certified doctors. Organizes schedules into distinct time slots, accounts for existing bookings, and dynamically removes occupied intervals.
6. Appointment Scheduling & Conflict Engine
Executes atomic booking transactions. Binds patient profiles, doctor records, dates, and slots. Strictly enforces uniqueness constraints to prevent concurrent double-booking anomalies.
7. Patient Records & Consultation History
Maintains patient health records, historical appointment statuses (Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled), reported symptoms, and AI triage summaries for longitudinal continuity of care.
8. Doctor Dashboard & Verification Portal
A specialized portal for medical practitioners to review upcoming queues, inspect AI preliminary summaries, add official clinical consultation notes, and verify treatment plans.

Clinical Safety, Security & Ethical Boundaries

🛡️ Medical Non-Replacement Directive
The AI engine is strictly designed as a clinical decision-support and triage tool. Every chatbot interaction explicitly incorporates standard disclaimers stating that AI guidance is preliminary and cannot replace the examination, diagnosis, or prescription of a qualified healthcare professional.
🔐 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Stateless JWT tokens encode cryptographically signed user roles (patient, doctor, admin). API route dependencies verify permissions, preventing unauthorized access across patient health records and doctor schedule configurations.
🔒 Data Privacy & Confidentiality
Passwords are encrypted using Bcrypt hashing with salted cost factors. In-transit data is secured via TLS/HTTPS, and patient symptom histories remain segregated within secure backend boundaries.
⚡ High-Availability & Fallback Design
If the AI similarity matcher encounters unstructured or highly ambiguous inputs below the confidence threshold (0.15), it safely routes the patient to a General Physician rather than guessing an unverified specialty.
