Identified Defects, Root Cause Analysis & Fixes

Defect 1 Slot Race Condition
RCA: Concurrently arriving booking requests read the slot as available before either completed the commit.
Fix: Implemented atomic validation locking ensuring that each slot check and reservation is serialized.
Defect 2 Hyphenated Clinical Terms
RCA: Tokenizer regex failed on medical phrases containing hyphens (e.g. shortness-of-breath).
Fix: Refactored tokenization regex to standardize punctuation and treat hyphenated clinical tokens as discrete words.
Defect 3 Dual-Auth Account Duplicate
RCA: Patients logging in first via Google and later via Phone OTP created separate fragmented profiles.
Fix: Enhanced auth.py to unify user identity records based on verified phone and email bindings.
Defect 4 Mobile Disclaimer Visibility
RCA: On narrow mobile screens (320px), the statutory medical disclaimer was pushed off-screen.
Fix: Redesigned the appointment modal footer with sticky positioning and responsive auto-scrolling.

Finalized Patient & Doctor Portals

🧑 Patient Health Portal
  • Active Appointments: Displays doctor name, clinic address, appointment date, time slot, and status.
  • AI Triage History: Full historical ledger of symptoms submitted and preliminary guidance received.
  • Appointment Management: Provides cancellation and rescheduling actions prior to appointment time.
  • Medical Disclaimers: Prominently displayed advisory reminding patients of doctor verification requirements.
👨‍⚕️ Doctor Clinical Dashboard
  • Real-Time Patient Queue: Chronological consultation roster organized by date and time slots.
  • AI Preliminary Clinical Summary: Instant pre-consultation intelligence including reported symptoms and matched condition.
  • Doctor Verification & Notes: Dedicated field for the physician to enter final diagnosis and prescription notes.
  • Consultation Status Lifecycle: Toggles status from Confirmed → In-Consultation → Completed.

User Manual & Deployment Quickstart

📖 Patient User Manual
  1. Login: Sign in via Google OAuth or enter mobile number for SMS OTP verification.
  2. AI Health Chat: Click the chat icon to describe symptoms in plain language.
  3. Select Doctor: Review recommended specialist discipline and choose a doctor.
  4. Book Slot: Pick date and available time slot, then click Confirm Booking.
  5. View Slip: Access confirmed appointment details in My Appointments.
📖 Doctor User Manual
  1. Provider Login: Log in with authorized clinical practitioner credentials.
  2. Review Roster: Inspect today's patient queue and view pre-consultation summaries.
  3. Review AI Summary: Evaluate patient-reported symptoms and suggested condition.
  4. Verify & Treat: Conduct examination, enter official notes, and mark consultation complete.
